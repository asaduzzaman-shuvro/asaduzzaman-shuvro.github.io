## Context

**The problem.** DN readers open the app to catch up fast — markets, breaking business news,
the morning read. A news app lives or dies on its feed: if scrolling stutters, images pop in
late, or the app crashes mid-article, readers go elsewhere.

## My role

I led the iOS team at Cefalo building the app for DN Media Group (formerly NHST Media Group),
owning architecture decisions, code quality, and delivery. DN is the flagship of the
[eight production apps](/#work) the team shipped for the group's brands, alongside TradeWinds,
Upstream, Fiskeribladet, IntraFish, Recharge and Europower.

As Staff Software Engineer I still build on it: Swift and SwiftUI features shipped with product
and design from discovery to release.

## Technical highlights

- **A feed that never hitches.** Ultra-smooth scrolling and optimized performance, so the
  feed stays seamless however far readers go.
- **Modular architecture.** Modularization and team-wide best practices cut release time
  by 60%.
- **Reliability as a feature.** A 99.3% crash-free rate across a 290K-download install base.
- **Sign-in and reading, in SwiftUI.** Sign-in and authentication flows, and an in-app PDF
  reader.

## Outcome

290K+ downloads, 30K+ daily and 200K+ weekly active users, and a 99.3% crash-free rate — plus a modular codebase
and release process that ships 60% faster.
