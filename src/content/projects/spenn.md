## Context

**The problem.** SPENN set out to make everyday payments cashless in Rwanda and Tanzania:
transfer money and pay digitally from a phone. When the app *is* the wallet, every transaction
has to be secure, every step has to be clear, and the app has to be there when people need it.

## My role

As a Software Engineer at Cefalo I built the SPENN iOS app, delivering business-critical
features from start to finish, inside the constraints of financial regulation.

## Technical highlights

- **Security built for finance.** Encryption, SSL pinning and advanced data-protection
  measures keep users' transactions safe and guard against attacks.
- **Step-by-step transaction flows.** Secure flows that walk people through each payment.
- **Compliance and availability.** Features shipped end to end in line with financial
  regulations, with high availability.

## Outcome

A payments app used by 30K people a week, launched in two countries — and the security
foundation I still bring to every app: SSL pinning, encryption and the OWASP Mobile Top 10.
