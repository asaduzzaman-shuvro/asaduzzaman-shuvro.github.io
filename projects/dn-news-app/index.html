DN — Norway's business news, on iOS

The iOS app for Dagens Næringsliv, Norway's leading business daily — a fast, hitch-free news feed for 15.2K daily and 90K+ weekly readers.

Role
iOS Team Lead
Team
iOS team at Cefalo, for NHST Media Group
Tech
Swift, SwiftUI, UIKit, Clean Architecture, Modularization, Fastlane
  • ▸ 252K+ downloads, 15.2K daily and 90K+ weekly active users.
  • ▸ 99% crash-free rate, with an ultra-smooth, hitch-free feed.
  • ▸ Modularization and shared practices cut release time by 60%.
App Store →

Context

The problem. DN readers open the app to catch up fast — markets, breaking business news, the morning read. A news app lives or dies on its feed: if scrolling stutters, images pop in late, or the app crashes mid-article, readers go elsewhere.

My role

I led the iOS team at Cefalo building the app for NHST Media Group, owning architecture decisions, code quality, and delivery. DN was one of eight production apps the team shipped for NHST’s brands, alongside TradeWinds, Upstream, Europower and Recharge.

Technical highlights

  • A feed that never hitches. Ultra-smooth scrolling and optimized performance, so the feed stays seamless however far readers go.
  • Modular architecture. Modularization and team-wide best practices cut release time by 60%.
  • Reliability as a feature. A 99% crash-free rate across a 252K-download install base.

Outcome

252K+ downloads, 15.2K daily and 90K+ weekly active users, and a 99% crash-free rate — plus a modular codebase and release process that ships 60% faster.
